# TennisDuel Design System

## Purpose

TennisDuel should feel like a **grand medieval tennis tournament**.

The UI should combine the excitement of competitive tennis with the atmosphere
of medieval tournaments, royal courts, and fantasy arenas.

## Visual Identity

The overall aesthetic should be:

- Medieval and tournament-inspired
- Prestigious and dramatic
- Competitive and immersive
- Polished rather than gimmicky

Draw inspiration from:

- Medieval heraldry and tournament banners
- Parchment and manuscripts
- Stone, wood, leather, and forged metal
- Royal courts and tournament arenas

## Colour

Prefer a rich, restrained palette inspired by medieval materials:

- Dark stone/charcoal
- Parchment
- Antique gold
- Burgundy/deep red
- Deep greens and blues
- Bronze and muted metallic tones

Avoid excessive neon, highly saturated colours, or generic modern
dashboard palettes.

## Typography

Use a distinctive, elegant display font for major headings where appropriate,
paired with a clean, highly readable font for body text and statistics.

Decorative typography should enhance the medieval atmosphere without reducing
readability.

## UI Style

Components should feel like they belong to the same medieval tournament
environment.

Use subtle references to:

- Heraldry
- Engraved metal
- Ornamental borders
- Parchment
- Tournament insignia

Avoid excessive decoration. **Clarity and usability always take priority over
theme.**

## Interaction

Important interactions should feel dramatic and rewarding.

The primary match-prediction action is:

**DUEL**

The DUEL interaction should feel like the moment two champions enter the
arena, with tasteful animation and visual emphasis.

## Consistency

Every new page and component should feel like part of the same TennisDuel
world.

Do not introduce a new visual style for an individual feature without a
deliberate design-system decision.

Exact colours, fonts, spacing, components, and design tokens should be
established here as the visual system evolves.
